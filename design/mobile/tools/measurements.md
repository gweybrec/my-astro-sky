# Revision 4 (placeholder colour, decided by the user: "very slightly lighter, don't make it too light")

Before: `.mob-field::placeholder` and `.mob-search::placeholder` were `text-dim` (#5a6b7d), 3.38:1 on `bg-input`. `text-label` (#6b7d8f) was tried first: 4.37:1 on `bg-input` (4.34:1 over the sheet ground), under the 4.5 to 5.0 range, so not used.
